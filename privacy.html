Privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-11-16

DK-Ledger is a personal tool used only by its owner. It has no other users.

What it accesses

  • The one Google Sheet it is attached to, where the ledger is stored.
  • Google Drive files that the app itself creates (receipt photos).
  • Gmail messages from senders or labels the owner chooses, to read invoices.

Where data goes

  • Data stays in the owner's own Google account.
  • Receipt images and chosen invoice emails are sent to Google Cloud Vertex AI to read amounts and items.
  • No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Removing data

The owner can delete the sheet and the receipt folder at any time, and can revoke the app's access in the Google Account security settings.
